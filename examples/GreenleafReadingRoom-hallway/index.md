# Greenleaf entrance

Fictional authored local fixture. Original CC0 text and box geometry.

Walk north through the open frame. Follow the narrow hallway, turn right,
then turn left through the second opening into the wider reading room.
These interior openings do not teleport. Walk back along the same route.

E reads a nearby aimed green marker. Tab selects nearby markers; Escape closes.
T reskins without moving you. R explicitly resets to this entry.
The south portal also resets entry; it is not the hallway passage.
